One class on `body` swaps the whole structure. The colors stay. Part of [[Ardentia]].

## Islands

Rounded panels floating over the abyss, 8 px apart. Tabs become pills and the status bar becomes a capsule in the corner.

## Continuous

Obsidian's native structure, panels joined and 1 px dividers.

## Focus

Ribbon, tabs, note header and status bar fade out until the mouse comes close.
